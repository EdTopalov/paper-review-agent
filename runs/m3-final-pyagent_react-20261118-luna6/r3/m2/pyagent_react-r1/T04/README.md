AgentBench contributes a broad interactive benchmark spanning eight text-accessible environments and reports results for 25 LLMs. Its practical task coverage and executable evaluation setup make it a useful research resource; the reported model comparisons are informative, but coverage, task-generation choices, and limited inspection of the paper’s detailed analysis constrain confidence in broad generalizations.

- **strength**: The benchmark brings together varied interactive settings rather than evaluating agents in only one environment.

- **strength**: The OS tasks use executable environments and checks, supporting objective assessment of task completion.
  - Page 6 · citation: exact

- **limitation**: A portion of OS tasks is GPT-4-generated, which is a relevant dataset-construction choice; its effect on task diversity or difficulty remains unclear from the inspected passage.
  - Page 5 · citation: whitespace
  - Quote: "about half of our instructions are created or collected from humans, while the other half are mostly QA problems generated by gpt-4"

- **strength**: The reported results show strong performance by GPT-4 across most environments, while success remains uneven across tasks.
  - Page 14 · citation: whitespace
  - Quote: "gpt-4 presents the best performance on 7 out of 8 datasets in AgentBench"

Unresolved questions:

- How robust are model rankings to alternative prompts, sampling settings, and repeated trials?
- How representative are the selected tasks of real-world agent use, and how do generated tasks compare with human-authored ones?
